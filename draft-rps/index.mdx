A standard 52-card deck, four suits read as **Rock, Paper, Scissors,** and **Trump**, each carrying a value from 2 to Ace.

[**Play it →**](./play.html) — pass-and-play on one screen, or online over a room code with a friend.

Each round of the match is a _pack_. Thirteen cards are dealt face-up; players alternate picks, taking 5 each and leaving 3, with the starting player alternating per pack. Once a pack is drafted, you play those five cards out — one each per turn, revealed together, five turns — then move to the next pack.

You draft in the open, but once play begins your opponent's hand is yours to remember. Four packs, most turns won takes the match. No ties.

## How a clash resolves

- Different RPS suits → Rock > Scissors > Paper > Rock (value ignored)
- Same suit → higher value wins
- Trump vs. other → trump wins if its value ≥ theirs
- Trump vs. trump → the lower value wins
